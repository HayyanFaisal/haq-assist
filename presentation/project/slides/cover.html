Haq Assist

Know your rights — with the law’s own words, in your language.

Ask in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English, typed or spoken. Get a plain answer in Urdu and English, read aloud if you like, the excerpts of Pakistani law it relies on, and a link to the official PDF page — or an honest “Not found”.

Introduce the team and the one idea: people see the legal text an answer relies on, with a link they can check, and the app says “Not found” when it can’t support an answer. It is a local prototype running on our own PC; for the demo we reach it over a private Tailscale link.
