Architecture

Five stages, with checks before anything is shown

1 · Understand

Gemma 4 12B finds topic and province, rewrites in legal English, writes 3 statute-style search sentences

2 · Retrieve

54 acts, 4,716 sections; that region’s law; each angle searched; fused search + reranker

3 · Answer

Steps in Urdu and English, each citing one source and one excerpt

4 · Check

Excerpt matched to the official text; numbers in both languages; a support pass

5 · Show

Trust level, excerpts, PDF page links, the live trace; read aloud on request

Nothing passes the checks?

Online search on: official .gov.pk / .gob.pk pages, labelled “not yet reviewed”. Off, or nothing found: “Not found” and a suggestion to see a lawyer.

Runs on one PC

Three local services: llama.cpp (Gemma), the FastAPI app, VoiceStudio (Whisper in, OmniVoice out). 13.4 of 16 GB on GPU 1.

Stage 4 is where safety lives: the excerpt must match the official text (a close paraphrase is replaced by the official words and checked again), every number must appear in it in English and Urdu, and a second pass of the same model confirms support and fails closed. For FIR, arrest, stop and search, online harm and traffic, the reviewed rights-card law is always among the sources. The live trace shows each stage as it happens and can be turned off in Settings.
