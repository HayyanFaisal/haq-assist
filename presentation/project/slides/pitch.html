What Haq Assist does

Ask about your problem in your own words. Get the law that applies, in plain Urdu, with its exact text.

1

You ask

In Roman Urdu, by voice or typing, exactly as on the last slide: “munshi keh raha hai pehle khud dhoondo…”

2

You get the law

The police must record a report of a crime like theft (CrPC s. 154); if they refuse, a Justice of the Peace can order them to (s. 22-A). Each step quotes the law and links to the government PDF.

3

It knows its limits

“Must I unlock my phone for the police?” It can’t verify an answer from the laws in its library, so it says “Not found, please ask a lawyer.”

SCRIPT: That’s what Haq Assist does, in one sentence: ask about your problem in your own words, and get the law that applies, in plain Urdu, with its exact text. So you type, or say, exactly what the clerk told you, in Roman Urdu. The app tells you that the police must record a report of a crime like theft, under section 154 of the Criminal Procedure Code, and that if they refuse, a Justice of the Peace can order them to, under section 22-A. Every step quotes the law word for word and links to the page of the government PDF, so you can check it yourself. And it knows its limits. Ask whether you must unlock your phone for the police, and it can’t verify an answer from its library, so instead of guessing it says “Not found, please ask a lawyer.”
