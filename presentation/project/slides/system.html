SCRIPT: For a generative AI system, the key requirements are about what the AI may say; the course terms are in brackets. Hallucination: never show a made-up law. Result: zero. Abstention: say “Not found” instead of guessing. It refused all 8. Retrieval: 94 percent in the final app, lower than the 98 on the earlier slide because three questions gave no province. Then two honest gaps, shown in the charts. Fairness, top chart: English got the right law every time, Roman Urdu only 12 of 17, likely because words like “parcha” or “thanedar” never appear in the law. Latency, bottom chart: Gemma alone takes 6.5 seconds, the look-up brings it to 12.3, and the check adds 1.5 more, so 13.8, just over our dashed 12-second target. We accept that cost, because the check is what removes made-up laws. Finally, privacy: everything runs on our own PC.
