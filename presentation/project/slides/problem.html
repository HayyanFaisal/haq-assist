Sources: Law & Justice Commission of Pakistan, case statement July–December 2025 (balance 2,222,042; district judiciary 1,904,043; Islamabad district 51,225); Census 2023.

SCRIPT: Here is the problem. Pakistan’s courts had 2.22 million cases pending at the end of 2025, and 86 percent of them are in district courts. Islamabad’s district courts alone had more than 51,000. At the same time, literacy is about 61 percent, and our laws are written in formal legal English. But many everyday problems never need a court. A faulty phone, a landlord keeping a deposit, police refusing to register an FIR: often the first step is a written notice or a complaint to the right office. The hard part is knowing which law applies to you and what to do next. That is the gap we work on.
