Organisations (legal-aid societies, NGOs, legal clinics) license it on their own server. Lawyers are listed free: we dropped the proposal’s 10–15% referral fee in light of rule 136.

Prices are proposals; the prototype counts questions but takes no payment. Pakistan Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Rules 1976, rules 135–136 (text checked).

Accuracy is never a paid feature: every plan gets the same checked answers, and rights cards are free. Rule 136 restricts paying for work and sharing fees with an unlicensed person, so referral commissions are out; rule 135 limits what a directory listing may contain. The demo directory uses fictional profiles. The demo voice, OmniVoice, is licensed for non-commercial use only.
