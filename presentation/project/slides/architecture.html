How it works

The pipeline: five steps from question to answer

SCRIPT: Here is the whole pipeline, with our stolen-phone question going left to right. The question is typed, or spoken and turned into text by Whisper. One, understand: Gemma 4, on our own PC, rewrites “FIR nahi kaat rahe” as “police refusing to register a theft report” and notes the province; for long stories it also writes law-style sentences to search with, called HyDE. Two, look up: two embedding models search by meaning, a reranker re-reads the best thirty and keeps six, here including CrPC 154 and 22-A. For urgent topics like arrest, rights cards we checked in advance join the sources. Three, answer: Gemma writes simple steps, each quoting one section, in a strict format at temperature zero. Four, the step that makes it safe: check. Every quote, number and province is compared with the official text, and a second AI pass confirms the quote supports the advice. A step that fails is removed; if nothing is left, it says “Not found” and points to a lawyer. Five, show the answer with its PDF page and a Listen button.
