Architecture

Five stages, with checks before anything is shown

1 · Understand

Gemma 4 12B finds topic and province, rewrites in legal English, writes 3 statute-style search sentences

2 · Retrieve

54 acts, 4,716 sections; that region’s law; each angle searched; fused search + reranker

3 · Answer

Steps in Urdu and English, each citing one source and one excerpt

4 · Check

Excerpt matched to the official text; numbers in both languages; a support pass

5 · Show

Trust level, excerpts, PDF page links, the live trace; read aloud on request

Nothing passes the checks?

Online search on: official .gov.pk / .gob.pk pages, labelled “not yet reviewed”. Off, or nothing found: “Not found” and a suggestion to see a lawyer.

Runs on one PC

Three local services: llama.cpp (Gemma), the FastAPI app, VoiceStudio (Whisper in, OmniVoice out). 13.4 of 16 GB on GPU 1.

SCRIPT: Here is how one question flows through the system. First, understand: Gemma works out the topic and the province, rewrites the question in legal English, and writes up to three statute-style search sentences. Second, retrieve: we search only the law that applies in that province, with two embedding models and a reranker, one search per angle, keeping up to six sections. Third, answer: Gemma writes steps in Urdu and English, and each step must cite one source and copy one exact quote. Fourth, the important one, check: code matches every quote against the official text, checks that every number in the English and the Urdu appears in the quote, and a second model pass confirms the quote supports the claim. If that pass errors, the step is rejected. Fifth, show: the trust level, the quotes, links to the PDF page, and a live trace of each stage. If nothing passes, the answer is “Not found”. With online search switched on, it can also try official government websites, and those answers go through the same checks. Everything runs as three services on one PC.
