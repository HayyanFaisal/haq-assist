SCRIPT: What exists today? A lawyer gives expert advice, but cost and waiting time vary. The official portals, Pakistan Code and the provincial codes, have the laws, but you have to find and interpret the right section yourself. NAZ Assist and JuriBot are legal chatbots, and general chatbots like ChatGPT speak fluent Urdu, but without grounding in the actual law they can cite sections that do not exist. We are not saying these tools have nothing. Our contribution is the combination: plain Urdu and English, the exact legal text behind every step with a link to the official page, a visible trust level, an honest “Not found”, rights cards and complaint drafts, all running locally.
