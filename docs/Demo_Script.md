# Haq Assist: demo script (4–5 minutes)

**Before you record:**
1. Run `Project\start_haq.ps1` and wait about a minute.
2. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 and set the province picker in the header to **Islamabad**.
3. Leave the composer on **Library only** for parts 1–3.
4. In **Settings** (the gear icon):
   - keep **Show how Haq works** on;
   - choose a plan with enough questions, e.g. Haq Ultra;
   - or press **Reset this month's questions**, so the Free limit of 8 doesn't stop the recording.

## 0:00 – 0:20 Hook

> "2.22 million cases are pending in Pakistan's courts. Most everyday problems never need a court. People need to know which law protects them, in their own language. That's Haq Assist."

## 0:20 – 1:20 Arrest in Islamabad

1. Optional: tap the mic and say it in Roman Urdu.
2. Type: `Police ne pakra hai, kehte hain raat thane mein rakhenge. Kitni der rakh sakte hain?` and press **Enter**.
3. While it works, point at the live pipeline on the right:
   > "Gemma reads it. Then it searches 4,716 sections, one angle at a time. You can see which area of law each search lands in. Now it writes, and now every quote is checked."
4. Then point at:
   - the **trust badge** and the **match confidence**;
   - **each step's exact quote**;
   - an **"Official PDF, page …"** link, which you open to show it's the government PDF.
5. Press **Listen (Urdu)** at the top right of the answer. Press it again to stop.
   > "It reads out only the checked answer, never the model's draft."
6. Open **Sources** (act, section, where it applies), **How this answer was checked**, and **How this answer was found** (the same pipeline, kept with the answer).
7. Open the linked **"Arrested or held at a police station"** rights card: pre-checked, it works without the AI, and every step is in Urdu too.

## 1:20 – 1:50 An honest limit

1. Ask: `Do I have to unlock my phone if police ask me to at a checkpoint?`
2. The answer is **Not found**.
   > "No settled rule in our library, so it refuses to guess and points to a lawyer."

## 1:50 – 2:20 People tell stories, not section numbers

1. Ask: `Bhai ka mobile G-9 markaz se chori ho gaya, thane gaye to munshi keh raha hai pehle khud dhoondo phir FIR karenge. 2 din se chakkar laga rahe hain. Ab kya karein?`
2. Point at what it now gives:
   - the duty to record the report (**CrPC 154**);
   - who can order the police (**CrPC 22-A**: Justice of the Peace);
   - the link to the reviewed **"Police won't register my FIR"** card.
   > "It splits a story into rights, remedy and offence and searches each one. It is still checked word for word."

## 2:20 – 3:20 Faulty phone: Islamabad vs Punjab

1. Go to **Complaint** and choose **Islamabad**.
2. Paste: `Maine 3 November ko Blue Area ki ABC Mobiles se 85,000 ka phone liya, 2 hafte mein screen kharab ho gayi, dukandar wapis nahi le raha. Mujhe refund chahiye. Mera naam Ali Raza hai.`
3. Click **Fill the form from my story**.
   > "It extracted the facts. My address stays blank, because nothing is invented."
4. Click **Draft my documents**. The result is a complaint to the Court of Sessions under §8, with the verified quotes and page links.
5. Switch to **Punjab**. The result changes to a **legal notice under §28**, which is required first, with a 15-day reply period and a 30-day filing deadline, all quoted.

## 3:20 – 3:45 Lawyer directory

1. Go to **Lawyers**, filter Islamabad + Consumer, and click **Send my case summary**.
2. Show the consent checkbox.
   > "Lawyers are listed free: Bar Council rules forbid referral fees."

## 3:45 – 4:30 Proof and close

1. Turn the **online switch ON** and ask: `What is the income tax rate for freelancers in Pakistan?`
   - If an official .gov.pk source answers, point at the **"Found online — not yet reviewed"** label.
   - If not, it says **Not found**, and both outcomes are correct behaviour.
2. Close:
   > "Every answer shows where it came from. When we can't prove it, we say so."

**If something fails live,** use a pre-recorded clip of the same steps. In the recording, show the **search-ladder chart** and the **A/B/C table** from the slides.
