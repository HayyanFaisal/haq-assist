Answer to the instructor’s question: our data is the official statute text, not a training set; our labelled data is the test sets. The ablation shows what each layer buys: the model alone wrote 77 citations and not one quote was in the official text. Be upfront: these are single runs, and LLM output varies by a few questions between runs. Excluded: 3 documents (broken text or the wrong law behind the title) and the Limitation Act’s deadline table, whose columns extract out of order. Punjab, Sindh and KP portals are all .gov.pk; only Balochistan uses .gob.pk.
