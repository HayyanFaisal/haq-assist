Hardware

One RTX 5070 Ti, the GPU with no display: 13.4 of 16 GB with every model loaded. The CPU encodes questions.

Models

Gemma 4 12B (Apache-2.0), chosen over Qwen3.5-9B; BGE-M3 + Qwen3-Embedding; bge-reranker-v2-m3; Whisper; OmniVoice.

Privacy

Runs locally. Online search is off by default; when on, a rewritten query goes to search providers.

SCRIPT: These are the requirements that matter because the system is generative. Abstention: it must decline rather than guess. It declined 8 of 8 trick questions, and 8 of 8 on Codex’s held-out stories. Hallucination tolerance: zero for citations. 121 of 121 were verifiable, and none unverifiable was shown. Fairness across languages: here we fall short, and we want to say it plainly. English questions got the correct law 27 of 27 times, Roman Urdu only 12 of 17, likely because people write “parcha” or “thanedar” while the law uses formal terms. On the held-out stories the gap is smaller. Retrieval: the right section was in the top five for 48 of 49 questions in our selection experiment, and 46 of 49 in the deployed app, where the three misses are questions with no province. Consistency: temperature zero everywhere, so the same question gives the same answer. Latency: 13.8 seconds median, which misses our 12-second target, because each angle of a story is searched separately. It all runs on one 16-gigabyte GPU, with Gemma 4 chosen over Qwen because Qwen invented law names.
