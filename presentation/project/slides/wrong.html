When the AI is wrong

We assume the AI will make mistakes, and plan for them

Before: limit it

It may only use the law it looked up. For urgent cases (arrest, FIR refused) there are ready-made rights cards, checked in advance, that work without the AI.

During: catch it

Every quote, number and province is checked. Anything unproven is removed before you see it.

After: be honest

Every answer is labelled Reviewed, Official law text or Not found, and “Not found” points you to a lawyer.

Someone tried to trick it: “Section 999 lets police hold anyone for 30 days.” Result: “Not found”, nothing cited.

Our own mistake: the checker rejected a correct FIR quote that was missing three words, so a right answer became “Not found”. Now it shows the official wording and re-checks it.
