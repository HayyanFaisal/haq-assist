SCRIPT: For a RAG system, the data is the law itself. We downloaded 54 laws, 4,716 sections, only from the five official government law websites, covering police and FIRs, arrest, consumer rights, rent, family, online harm, land and traffic. Every section keeps its PDF page, which is how each quote links back to its source. We train nothing, so what we needed was a fair test: 57 questions with known answers, including 8 the app should refuse, and 40 long, messy real-life stories written by another AI, Codex, which we didn’t read until the final test. Here is the key result, an ablation test: the same AI, Gemma 4, with and without our system. Alone, it gave 77 citations, and not one quote was in the actual law. With Haq Assist, 121 of 121 quotes are real, the answer cites the right law in 41 of 49 questions, and it refuses all 8 it should. The unseen stories are harder: right law in 19 of 32, and all 8 refusals correct.
