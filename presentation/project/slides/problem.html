SCRIPT: Imagine your brother’s phone is stolen. You go to the police station, and the clerk says: go and find it yourself first, then we’ll register the FIR. Is that allowed? Who can make them register it? Most of us have no idea. Pakistan’s courts already have more than two million cases waiting, our laws are written in formal legal English, and yet many everyday problems never need a court: the first step is often a complaint or a written notice, if you know which law to use. So the gap is simple: people don’t know what the law says about their own situation. We’ll follow this stolen phone through the whole talk.
