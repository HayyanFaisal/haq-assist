SCRIPT: How could this be sustained? Our rule is that accuracy is never a paid feature: every plan gets the same checked answers, and the rights cards are free. Free gives 8 questions a month. Haq Go is 149 rupees for 40, Haq Plus 599 for 80, and Haq Ultra 899 for 150. Organisations such as legal-aid societies, NGOs and university legal clinics could license it to run on their own server. Our proposal had a 10 to 15 percent referral fee from lawyers. We dropped it, because Bar Council rule 136 restricts sharing fees with someone who is not a lawyer, so lawyers are listed free. These prices are proposals: the prototype counts questions but takes no payment.
