SCRIPT: For a generative AI system, the key requirements are about what the AI may say. In brackets are the course terms. Hallucination: never show a made-up law. Target zero, result zero. Abstention: say “Not found” instead of guessing. It refused all 8. Retrieval: the right section was found for 94 percent of questions in the final app. That’s lower than the 98 percent on the last slide because three questions gave no province, so the app searched only nationwide law or asked which province. Two honest gaps. Fairness: English questions got the right law every time, Roman Urdu only 12 of 17, most likely because words like “parcha” or “thanedar” never appear in the law; we added an everyday-word dictionary, but it isn’t solved. And latency: a median of 13.8 seconds, a little over our target. Finally, privacy: everything runs on our own PC.
