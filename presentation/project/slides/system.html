Hardware

One RTX 5070 Ti, the GPU with no display: 13.4 of 16 GB with every model loaded. The CPU encodes questions.

Models

Gemma 4 12B (Apache-2.0), chosen over Qwen3.5-9B; BGE-M3 + Qwen3-Embedding; bge-reranker-v2-m3; Whisper; OmniVoice.

Privacy

Runs locally. Online search is off by default; when on, a rewritten query goes to search providers.

Targets come from our proposal. Fairness is the honest weak spot: Roman Urdu questions were cited correctly less often than English ones, likely because everyday words differ from legal wording; the glossary and statute-style search sentences target this. Urdu-script samples are too small to claim parity. Every plan gets the same verified answers. The online fallback adds 18–40 seconds.
