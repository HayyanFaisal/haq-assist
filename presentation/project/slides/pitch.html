SCRIPT: In one sentence: Haq Assist finds the Pakistani law that applies to you, explains it in Urdu, shows you the exact excerpt, and tells you when it cannot. Take Ali, a student in Islamabad, stopped at a checkpoint at night and taken to the police station. He asks by voice, in Urdu. The speech is transcribed on our PC, and he can watch the system read his question, search the law and check each quote. He gets Article 10 of the Constitution and section 61 of the Criminal Procedure Code: he must be produced before a magistrate within 24 hours, not counting travel time. Each step is quoted with its PDF page, and he can have it read aloud. Then he asks whether he must unlock his phone. There is no settled rule in our library, so the answer is “Not found”, see a lawyer. Later, when a new phone breaks, the app drafts a consumer complaint for Islamabad, or the legal notice that Punjab requires first.
