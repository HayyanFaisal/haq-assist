BACKUP SLIDE: show it if you are asked which laws are in the library. SCRIPT: What is actually in the library? These are the acts behind the problems people ask about most. For arrest, the Constitution’s Article 10. For the police and FIRs, the Criminal Procedure Code: section 154 is the FIR, and under 22-A a Justice of the Peace can order the police to register it. The Police Order covers police misconduct, and PECA covers online harassment and blackmail. For a faulty product, the Islamabad and Punjab consumer laws: in Islamabad you complain directly, while in Punjab you must send the seller a notice first. That difference is exactly why the app asks for your province. Then family law, the Islamabad rent law, and for land and inheritance the Specific Relief Act and the women’s property laws. In total there are 54 acts, deepest for Islamabad and federal law, plus each province’s consumer, rent, police, traffic and harassment laws. The full list is in our repository.
