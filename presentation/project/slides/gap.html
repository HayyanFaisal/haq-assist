Haq Assist combines plain Urdu and English explanations, the statutory excerpts behind them with official PDF page links, a visible trust level, an explicit “Not found”, Islamabad rights cards and complaint drafts — in one app that runs locally.

We describe what each option offers rather than claiming what they lack: other tools exist, including ones with citations. Our contribution is the combination, the checks, and the honest “Not found”.
