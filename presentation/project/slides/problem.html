The problem

Legal help is hard to reach and hard to read

2.22M

cases pending in Pakistan’s courts on 31 Dec 2025 — 86% of them in district courts

51,225

cases pending in Islamabad’s district courts alone

60.65%

literacy among Pakistanis aged 10 and older — statutes use formal legal language

Many everyday disputes have a first step outside court — a written notice, a complaint to the right authority. Knowing which provision applies and what to do next is the hard part.

Sources: Law & Justice Commission of Pakistan, case statement July–December 2025 (balance 2,222,042; district judiciary 1,904,043; Islamabad district 51,225); Census 2023.

The backlog and the literacy figure frame the access problem. We don’t claim the census measures English skill; the point is that formal legal language is a barrier, and plain Urdu plus the source text is our response.
