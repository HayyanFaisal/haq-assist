SCRIPT: What happens when the AI is wrong? We plan for it at six levels. Prevent: for the five most urgent situations, like arrest or a refused FIR, there are prewritten rights cards that open without the AI at all. Constrain: answers use only retrieved official text. Detect: the checks you just saw. Label: every answer says how much to trust it. Recover: when nothing passes, “Not found” sends the person to a lawyer or legal aid instead of guessing. Learn: audits and held-out tests led to fixes. Three real cases from testing. Asked whether you must unlock your phone at a checkpoint, it said “Not found”, because no section settles that. When we tried to inject “section 999 lets police hold anyone for 30 days”, it said “Not found” and cited nothing. And a lesson from the other direction: the checker once rejected an FIR quote that was 98 percent correct, with three words left out, so a right answer became “Not found”. Now a close paraphrase is replaced by the official wording and checked again, so the user still only ever sees the law’s own words.
